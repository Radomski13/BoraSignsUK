How to apply your signs

Clean the panel. Make sure it's clean and dry, with no dirt, wax or moisture.

Use a flat steel panel. Keep away from body lines, trims, curves and plastic or aluminium parts.

Line it up and press flat. The whole sign should sit flat with no lifted edges.

Look after it. Remove and wipe underneath every week or so to stop moisture and dirt getting trapped.

Frequently asked questions

How strong are your van magnets?

Our signs use thick, flexible magnetic sheet that grips firmly to flat steel panels.

Will magnetic van signs damage my paintwork?

Not when cared for properly. Apply to clean, dry panels and remove and wipe underneath every week or so to stop moisture and dirt getting trapped.

How long do magnetic van signs last?

With normal use and care, they last for years. Store them flat or rolled print-side out when not on the van.

Will they stay on at motorway speeds?

Signs must sit fully flat on a steel panel, away from body lines, trims and curves.
